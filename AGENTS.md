# Acceptance Capsule Procurement Service

This settlement owns one operation: purchase competing acceptance-oracle
proposals through the inference-work market and submit an exact candidate to
the separately owned protected-acceptance assay. It does not review itself,
implement software, select providers for customers, or accept usefulness.
The complete supplier competition is declared and dispatched before any
result is examined. A refusal remains a failed contact in that competition;
it never causes a replacement lot.
